# NovaCast Studio — Execution Playbook

## Role and operating principles

Qualify and serve B2B buyers for paid AI UGC creative pilots and monthly production. Keep the positioning premium, minimalist, and performance-driven. The landing page is English-only; Gulf Arabic/Khaleeji is a production option confirmed per project. The only landing-page conversion action is WhatsApp.

- Sell a scoped paid Starter pilot first, then recommend Growth or Retainer only when volume and workflow justify it.
- Describe creative as designed for paid-media testing; do not promise ad performance.
- Do not introduce other website contact routes or capture flows.
- The page link currently uses `https://wa.me/YOUR_PHONE_NUMBER?text=...`; replace the placeholder with the approved WhatsApp Business number before launch.

## WhatsApp intake

### First-response template

> Hi, thanks for reaching out to NovaCast Studio. We create hook-first AI UGC-style video ads for paid-media testing. To scope a paid Starter pilot, please share your product page, target market, current offer, brand guidance, and any claims or topics we should avoid. We’ll confirm asset readiness, dialect availability, scope, and a 48–72-hour delivery window after the brief and required assets are approved.

Use the prefilled WhatsApp message from the landing page as the initial opener. Keep subsequent intake in the same WhatsApp conversation unless the client requests another operational channel.

### Required intake questions

1. What is the product/store URL, and which SKU should the creative feature?
2. Which target geography and audience are you buying media for?
3. What offer, price point, or campaign objective should the video communicate?
4. Which hooks, angles, claims, or product representations are allowed or prohibited?
5. What is the brand voice, visual guidance, and required CTA?
6. Which competitor or reference ads should inform the direction? Ask for links and clarify that references are for direction, not copying.
7. Is English or a requested Arabic dialect needed? For GCC, confirm whether Khaleeji/Gulf Arabic is appropriate for the target market and can be staffed for this project.
8. Are approved product images, logos, brand assets, scripts, and likeness/voice permissions ready?

### Qualification rubric

Score each dimension **Ready / Needs clarification / Blocked** before quoting:

- **Budget:** Buyer understands this is a paid pilot and has a budget or requests a scoped quote. No unpaid speculative work.
- **Urgency:** Desired launch date and review availability are clear; confirm that the requested 48–72-hour window is feasible for the approved brief and available assets.
- **Asset readiness:** Product URL, approved assets, brand guidance, claim restrictions, and required permissions are available or have a named delivery date.

Proceed to a written scope when no dimension is Blocked. If blocked, explain the missing decision or asset and pause the SLA clock until resolved.

## Packaging and quoting

1. **Starter Test Pack:** 3 short-form videos, typically 15–35 seconds, with distinct hooks. Include captioned and clean-caption exports and the standard revision scope below. Target first delivery in 48–72 hours after brief approval and receipt of required assets; confirm the actual schedule before taking the order.
2. **Growth Creative Pack:** 8–12 videos per month, organized around an agreed hook/angle matrix and weekly delivery batches. Quote after confirming monthly volume, language, review cadence, and revision scope.
3. **Ad Machine Retainer:** 20–30 videos per month with ongoing planning and a creative backlog. Agree SLA, approvals, scope, and support cadence before kickoff.

Quote from deliverable count, complexity, language/dialect, asset readiness, revision scope, and SLA. State exclusions and commercial usage terms in the project agreement. Do not invent fixed prices, results, or unconditional deadlines.

## Production pipeline

1. **Brief:** Record objective, audience, market, product facts, approved assets, offer, CTA, prohibited claims, language, and placement.
2. **Concept approval:** Prepare hook and angle options plus script direction. Obtain client approval before production; apply the one included pre-production text/hook revision.
3. **Production:** Use the approved production workflow and only tools/accounts with authorized access. Popcorn Studio (`popcornstudio.ai`) may be used for its available generation workflow; Higgsfield API may be used for video generation; ElevenLabs may be used for voice where licensed and appropriate. Confirm current tool availability and project permissions before committing to a tool or delivery date. Do not list Wan 3.0 or Seedance 2.5 as standard tools unless their use is confirmed for the project.
4. **Human QA:** Review product depiction, continuity, pronunciation, dialect, pacing, captions, brand fit, claim compliance, and export integrity. Escalate any mismatch for client approval or regeneration.
5. **Delivery:** Share agreed files through the project’s approved delivery channel, then collect consolidated feedback within the agreed review window.

## Revision policy

- Standard scope: one text/hook revision before production and one style revision after delivery.
- A new angle, script, product, language, or scene is a new version and may change price and schedule.
- Record any different revision arrangement in the approved project scope before work starts.

## Legal and compliance checklist

- Product statements are supported by client-provided evidence and approved copy.
- No unsupported medical, financial, comparative, quantified, or before/after claims.
- No synthetic testimonial implying personal use, firsthand experience, or endorsement unless explicitly compliant, substantiated, and approved for the intended market and placement.
- Client has rights to supplied product assets, logos, music, likenesses, and voice references; generated voices and likenesses are properly licensed and disclosed where required.
- Khaleeji/Gulf dialect, pronunciation, script, and market suitability are confirmed with the client and a qualified reviewer when needed.
- Commercial usage rights, platform restrictions, and client approval responsibilities are stated in the project terms. Never guarantee platform acceptance.

## Delivery checklist

- Export agreed video count and aspect ratios; default paid-social master is vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 where supported by the approved production pipeline.
- Include burn-in captions and clean-caption version where scoped; verify text safe areas, spelling, timing, and legibility.
- Use consistent filenames, for example: `Brand_Product_Market_Angle_Hook01_9x16_EN_v01.mp4`.
- Include a short manifest with filename, duration, aspect ratio, language/dialect, caption version, and revision/version number.
- Confirm files open, audio is audible where intended, and no unapproved claims, assets, or tool marks remain.
- Delivery timing is 48–72 hours after approved brief and receipt of required assets only when confirmed feasible; feedback or missing assets can change the schedule.
