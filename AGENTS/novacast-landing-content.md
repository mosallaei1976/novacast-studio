### 8. Frequently Asked Questions (سوالات متداول)
* **Q: Will these ads look like obvious AI generated content?**
  * *A:* AI-generated content can have visible limitations and may not look identical to creator-shot footage. We review pacing, voice, and visuals and share samples so you can assess fit before ordering.
* **Q: What do I need to supply to get started?**
  * *A:* Just your product page URL, branding guidelines, and any specific claims or do-not-mention details. We handle scripts, avatars, voices, and editing from start to finish.
* **Q: Do I own the rights to the videos?**
  * *A:* Usage rights and any restrictions are stated in the project terms. The client must have permission to use supplied logos, product assets, likenesses, and voices; platform acceptance is not guaranteed.
* **Q: What is your standard turnaround time?**
  * *A:* Starter first delivery is targeted within 72 hours after the brief is approved. Growth is delivered in weekly batches; retainer timing follows the agreed SLA. Timing may depend on feedback and asset readiness.
* **Q: Can we request revisions?**
  * *A:* Revision scope is agreed before kickoff. The standard workflow allows one text/hook revision before production and one style revision after delivery; a new angle or scenario is scoped as a new version.
