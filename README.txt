NOVACAST STUDIO LANDING PAGE
===========================

PROJECT
-------
Single-page, mobile-first landing page for NovaCast Studio, an AI-powered
UGC-style video ad service for DTC eCommerce brands and growth marketing teams.

FILES
-----
index.html              Landing page markup, styling, and JavaScript.
llms.txt                Plain-language site information for AI assistants.
assets/                 Landing page images, social preview image, and brand mark.

RUN LOCALLY
-----------
Open index.html in a modern browser, or serve this folder with a local static
HTTP server. The page does not require a build step or package installation.

CONTENT AND PACKAGE NOTES
-------------------------
Starter Test Pack: 3 video ads and 3 hooks. First delivery is targeted within
72 hours after brief approval and required asset readiness.

Growth Creative Pack: 8-12 video ads per month with weekly delivery batches.

Ad Machine Retainer: 20-30 video ads per month. Timing follows the agreed SLA.

Pricing is provided after scope review. Commercial usage terms and revision
scope are agreed per project. Advertising results and platform acceptance are
not guaranteed. Portfolio images are AI-generated concept visuals, not client
work or performance claims.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
-----------------
- Deploy index.html and the complete assets/ directory together.
- Confirm novacaststudio.com is the final production domain and configure the
  canonical, Open Graph, Twitter, and JSON-LD URLs to match the live site.
- Replace temporary social links (href="#") with verified profile URLs.
- Connect the demo lead form to an approved form service or backend. At present,
  submission opens the visitor's email application with a prefilled message.
- Replace concept visuals with approved portfolio media when available.
- Review the page at actual mobile widths and measure Core Web Vitals after
  deployment.
