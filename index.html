FAQ

Good to know before you start.

Clear expectations help every creative project move faster.

AI-generated content can have visible limitations and may not look identical to creator-shot footage. We review pacing, voice, and visuals, and can share examples so you can assess fit.

Share your product page, target market, brand guidance, audience, and any claims or topics to avoid. We will confirm any extra assets needed before production.

Usage rights and restrictions are set out in the project terms. You are responsible for having permission to use supplied logos, product assets, likenesses, and voices. Platform acceptance is not guaranteed.

Starter first delivery is targeted within 48 hours after brief approval and asset readiness. Growth is delivered weekly; Retainer timing follows the agreed SLA. Feedback timing can affect the schedule.

Revision scope is agreed before kickoff. The standard workflow includes one text or hook revision before production and one style revision after delivery. A new angle or scenario is scoped as a new version.
